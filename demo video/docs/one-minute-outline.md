# Full film direction — proposed extension

The exported [15-second film](../exports/neurasign-style-sample-15s.mp4) is the implemented style sample: desk footage (0–3.5 s), wearable close-up (3.5–5 s), manager overview (5–8 s), heart-rate detail (8–11 s), then the logo (11–15 s). It uses recorded interface stills with editorial motion; it does not show a live connection or a continuously recorded product walkthrough.

The timeline below is a **proposed 60-second extension**, not an additional rendered composition. It should retain the sample's realistic footage, cobalt palette, large legible titles and restrained sound, with more time for actual product behavior.

| Time | Purpose | Picture and copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | Human opening | Work, wrist wearable. “Every team has a pulse.” |
| 7–15 s | Explain the system | One simple connection: Wearable → Phone → Team dashboard. Show the actual phone connector only if its state can be captured accurately. |
| 15–28 s | Demonstrate the overview | Actual dashboard. Show search/filter and move from a team overview to one person's detail. Keep recorded-demo context visible. |
| 28–41 s | Show what is measured | Real heart-rate, variability and other available signal charts. Show readable units and the separation between observations and experimental interpretations. |
| 41–51 s | Manager outcome | Return to manager view. Show the real review workflow if the recorded snapshot contains a review state; otherwise demonstrate navigation without fabricating an alert. |
| 51–60 s | Product close | Return to the real setting, then a strong logo hold. “Physiological intelligence for work.” |

Keep one clear idea in each shot. No numerical performance claims without their actual evaluation context. No footage of a supposed clinical intervention or a real emergency. The two demo profiles stay labeled as sample profiles; do not invent a hundred-person deployed customer to imply scale.

For an Astra-like human presence, team-shot phone footage can replace the licensed desk setting later. The current edit is already usable without filming or generative humans.

The existing original soundtrack is composed for the 15-second sample and resolves at its logo ending. Extend or recompose it for the longer edit; do not stretch it to a minute or treat the optional cut accents as an additional music layer. See the [audio cue sheet and provenance](audio-notes.md).
